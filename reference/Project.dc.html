Scale. Multiple halls running at once, a 35-person crew to hire, train and schedule across departments, and kit to source and move for all of them.

Months of pre-production: venue scouting and tech recces for each hall, equipment and logistics planning, crew hiring and scheduling, and a content plan agreed with stakeholders before day one.
